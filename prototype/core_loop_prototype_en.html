This is not a game; it's an experiment to answer three questions.
Play for 5 minutes first, then come back and read the questions below.
What This Prototype Tests
  1. Aim at specific parts versus just shooting the torso — can you feel the strategic difference?
  2. Does the heat mechanic create a "burst–pause" rhythm, or is it just annoying?
  3. Are the trade-offs among the three mech loadouts a genuine dilemma, or is there one clearly optimal solution?
