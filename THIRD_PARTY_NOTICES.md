## 1. Poly Haven textures — CC0

Source: `https://polyhaven.com/license` (API: `api.polyhaven.com`).

- Assets: `concrete_floor_02`, `concrete_wall_001`, `factory_wall`, `rusty_metal`,
  `corrugated_iron`, `metal_grate_rusty` (2K Diffuse/Normal/ARM JPGs).
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal** — effectively public domain. Commercial use,
  modification and redistribution are all permitted; attribution is not required
  (appreciated but optional).
- Status: safe to include and redistribute. Credit: Poly Haven (voluntary).

## 2. Hunyuan3D generated models — Tencent Hunyuan 3D 2.0 Community License

Source: `https://github.com/Tencent/Hunyuan3D-2` (model weights NOT included in
this repo; only generated outputs are).

- Assets: 4 mech-wreck GLBs / UE static meshes (`mech_torso_wreck`,
  `severed_mech_arm`, `mech_head_wreck`, `armor_debris`) and their texgen PBR
  textures, under `ue/Content/RobotGame/Art/Props/Gen3D/` and `docs/gen3d_shots/`.
- License: Tencent states it claims no rights in the **Output** you generate, and
  the generated models may be used commercially. However the Community License
  imposes use-based restrictions that are relevant when redistributing:
  - **Territory**: the license excludes the EU, United Kingdom and South Korea.
  - **No training other models**: outputs must not be used to improve other AI models.
  - **Scale threshold**: above 1 million monthly active users, a separate license
    from Tencent is required.
  - **Distribution**: must include a copy of the license and indicate the product
    is "Powered by Tencent Hunyuan" (per the license's distribution terms).
  - **AUP**: comply with Tencent's Acceptable Use Policy.
- Status: included for demonstration; the above restrictions are **not** covered
  by this repository's MIT License. If redistributing commercially, review the
  full license at `https://github.com/Tencent/Hunyuan3D-2/blob/main/LICENSE`.

## 3. Image-model outputs (posters / concept art) — model-dependent

- Assets: `art_src/posters/`, `art_src/textures/`, `art_src/textures_raw/`, and
  the AI-generated concept images referenced by the workflow docs.
- Generated with ComfyUI using `moss-image`, `krea2`, `z_image_turbo` and/or
  `flux` models (see `tools/gen_posters.py`, `tools/gen_textures.py`, etc.).
- **Action needed**: the redistribution rights of AI-generated images depend on
  the specific base model's license. Flux.1 [dev] is non-commercial; Flux.1
  [schnell] is Apache-2.0; z_image / krea2 / moss-image have their own terms.
  Confirm which model produced each image and its license before any commercial
  redistribution. These images are included for demonstration only.

## 4. Unreal Engine

- The repository contains a UE 5.8 **C++ source project** and `.uasset` **project
  content** (maps, meshes, materials). It does **not** contain the Unreal Engine
  binary/runtime or engine source.
- Unreal Engine itself is governed by the Epic Games Unreal Engine EULA; using
  or distributing the engine requires compliance with that agreement. The MIT
  License here does not apply to Unreal Engine or to Epic's IP.

## 5. Removed: Cosmos (Leartes Studios) — commercial, redistribution prohibited

- Previously the repo contained preview glTF models fetched from the public CDN
  `api.cosmos.leartesstudios.com` (e.g. `cs1-pipe`, `ww2-sandbag-stack`,
  `met-barrel01-set01`, etc.) and their UE imports.
- The Cosmos **Digital Asset Licensing Contract** states the license is
  non-exclusive, non-transferable and limited, requires a paid subscription /
  per-asset license, and explicitly prohibits redistributing the licensed
  content on a standalone basis. No such license was held.
- **Resolution**: all Cosmos model files, their UE assets, showcases and fetch
  scripts were removed from the repository and the corresponding actors were
  removed from the level. This is why the current repo contains no Cosmos content.

## 6. IdolMech model — provenance to confirm

- Asset: `art_src/idol/IdolMech.glb/.fbx` and the UE static meshes under
  `ue/Content/RobotGame/Art/Props/IdolMech*/`.
- This file was **provided by the project user**; its original source and license
  were not documented (filenames such as `herta_rgba.png` in the workflow suggest
  it may derive from a third-party character model).
- **Action needed**: confirm the model's source and redistribution rights. If it
  is a third-party IP model, remove it before public/commercial redistribution.
  (Its level actors were already removed; only the asset files remain.)

## 7. Excluded third-party tooling

The following are used locally during development but are **excluded from the
repository** via `.gitignore`: `tools/w64devkit/` (MinGW toolchain),
`tools/flipfluids_src/` (FLIP Fluids, GPLv3 / MIT / Standard Royalty Free —
multi-licensed), ComfyUI, Hunyuan3D venv and model weights. Their licenses remain
with their respective owners.
